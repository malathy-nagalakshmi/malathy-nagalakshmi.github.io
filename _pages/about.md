I am a Software Engineer at Meta, where I work on scalable and reliable infrastructure, focusing on privacy pipelines and storage compression. Previously, I worked at Amazon, gaining experience in cloud infrastructure and high-throughput streaming systems.

I graduated from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/) with a Master’s degree in Computer and Information Science in May 2023. During my time there, I had the opportunity to work with [Prof. Lyle Ungar](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~ungar/), [Prof. Chris Callison-Burch](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~ccb/), and [Prof. Lara Martin](https://laramartin.net/). My academic interests centered on Natural Language Processing and Computational Social Science, particularly at the intersection of language, human behavior, and psychology. This research informs my interest in AI safety, motivating my work to design resilient distributed systems that keep intelligent models reliable and transparent.

Previously, I collaborated with [Prof. Raghavan Komondoor](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~raghavan/) and [Dr. Raveendra Kumar Medicherla](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=S9gOtwkAAAAJ&hl=en) at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/). I received my Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) in Computer Science and Engineering from [PES University](https://cs.pes.edu/).
